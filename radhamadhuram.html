Kesar Pista Shrikhand

Prep Time15 mins
Cook Time0 mins
Total Time15 mins (+4h rest)
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Line a deep bowl with a clean muslin cheesecloth and pour yogurt in it.

2

Tie the cloth and hang it for 4 hours to drain all the excess liquid (whey), obtaining thick hung curd (Chakka).

3

Transfer hung curd into a mixing bowl, add powdered sugar and saffron milk infusion.

4

Whisk vigorously until completely smooth, glossy, and lump-free.

5

Stir in cardamom powder and half of the pistachios and almonds.

6

Serve in bowls topped with remaining nuts and saffron strands. Chill before offering.

Malpua with Rabdi

Prep Time20 mins
Cook Time30 mins
Total Time50 mins
Servings6 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Boil sugar, water, saffron, and cardamom to make a sticky 1-thread syrup. Keep warm.

2

Mix flour, semolina, fennel seeds, milk, and 1/2 cup rabdi to make a smooth pouring batter. Rest for 30 minutes.

3

Heat shallow ghee in a flat pan. Pour a small ladle of batter in the center to form a pancake.

4

Fry on medium-low heat until golden-brown on both sides, then drain excess ghee.

5

Immediately soak hot malpuas in sugar syrup for 2-3 minutes, then remove.

6

Serve drizzled with rich thick rabdi and garnished with sliced pistachios.

Gajar Ka Halwa

Prep Time20 mins
Cook Time40 mins
Total Time60 mins
Servings6 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Heat 2 tbsp ghee in a heavy kadhai, sauté grated carrots for 10 minutes until soft.

2

Pour in milk, mix well, and simmer on medium heat until milk evaporates completely (35 mins).

3

Add sugar and remaining ghee, stirring continuously until the halwa becomes glossy.

4

Fold in crumbled khoya (mawa) and cardamom powder, cooking for 5 more minutes.

5

Garnish with cashews and almonds fried in a little ghee. Serve warm.

Gulab Jamun

Prep Time20 mins
Cook Time20 mins
Total Time40 mins
Servings6 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Boil sugar, water, and cardamom to form syrup. Add rose water and keep warm.

2

Mash khoya and paneer together with your palm until smooth. Add flour, baking powder, and bind with milk to form a soft dough.

3

Shape into small smooth balls with no cracks on the surface.

4

Heat ghee in a wok on low heat. Deep fry the balls slowly, rotating them gently until dark golden brown.

5

Drain ghee, transfer immediately to hot sugar syrup, and let soak for 2 hours before serving.

Classic Masala Dosa

Prep Time15 mins (+ferment)
Cook Time30 mins
Total Time45 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Prepare Potato Masala: Sauté mustard seeds, curry leaves, onions, green chili, ginger, and turmeric in oil. Fold in boiled mashed potatoes, salt, and water. Cook for 5 minutes. Set aside.

2

Heat a flat cast iron skillet (tawa), splash water to cool it, and wipe clean.

3

Pour a ladle of batter in the center, spread in a circular spiral motion outward to make a thin crepe.

4

Drizzle ghee around the edges and cook on medium-high until the base turns golden and crispy.

5

Place a portion of potato masala in the center, fold the dosa over it, and serve hot with sambar and coconut chutney.

Ghee Aloo Paratha

Prep Time15 mins
Cook Time15 mins
Total Time30 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Knead wheat flour with water and a pinch of salt to form a soft dough. Rest for 15 mins.

2

Mix mashed potatoes, ginger-chili paste, ajwain, garam masala, chili powder, coriander, and salt together to make the stuffing.

3

Roll a small ball of dough into a 4-inch circle, place a spoonful of stuffing in the center, gather edges to seal, and roll back into a ball.

4

Gently roll the stuffed dough ball into a flatbread, taking care that the filling does not tear out.

5

Cook on a hot tawa. Flip when bubbles appear, spread ghee on both sides, and press with a spatula until golden-brown spots appear.

6

Serve hot with white butter, fresh curd, and pickles.

Soft Steamed Idli

Prep Time10 mins (+ferment)
Cook Time20 mins
Total Time30 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Grease idli molds with oil, fill with ladlefuls of fermented rice-urad batter.

2

Steam in an idli steamer on high heat for 10-12 minutes until soft and cooked. Cool and demold.

3

Sambar: Boil veggies in tamarind water. Add boiled dal, sambar powder, and salt. Cook for 10 mins.

4

Sambar Tempering: Crackle mustard seeds, curry leaves, and asafoetida in ghee, pour into hot sambar.

5

Serve piping hot soft idlis dipped in sambar with a side of white coconut chutney.

Indori Kanda Poha

Prep Time10 mins
Cook Time10 mins
Total Time20 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Wash the Poha in a colander until soft. Sprinkle salt, sugar, and turmeric powder over it. Toss lightly and drain completely.

2

Heat 1 tbsp oil, fry peanuts until crunchy, and set aside.

3

In the same oil, add mustard seeds and green chili. Add chopped onions and sauté until translucent.

4

Add poha and peanuts, mix gently, cover and steam on very low heat for 3-4 minutes.

5

Remove lid, add lemon juice and coriander. Garnish with sev and pomegranate seeds before serving.

Paneer Butter Masala

Prep Time15 mins
Cook Time20 mins
Total Time35 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Heat 1 tbsp butter and oil, sauté ginger-garlic paste and spices for a minute.

2

Add tomato-cashew puree and cook on medium flame until oil starts leaving the sides (10 mins).

3

Pour in 1 cup warm water, mix well, and simmer to a thick gravy.

4

Add paneer cubes, remaining butter, salt, and simmer for 3 minutes on low heat.

5

Finish with crushed kasuri methi, garam masala, and fresh cream. Serve with butter naan.

Dal Makhani & Jeera Rice

Prep Time15 mins (+8h soak)
Cook Time35 mins
Total Time50 mins
Servings5 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Pressure cook soaked dal and kidney beans with salt and water for 5-6 whistles until soft.

2

Sauté ginger-garlic paste, red chili, and tomato puree in butter. Cook until fat separates.

3

Add cooked dal, mash slightly with ladle, and simmer on low heat for 25 minutes to develop creamy richness.

4

Jeera Rice: Sauté cumin seeds in ghee. Add washed basmati rice, water, and salt. Cook until grains are separate and fluffy.

5

Stir cream and remaining butter into dal. Serve piping hot with jeera rice.

Punjabi Chole Bhature

Prep Time20 mins (+8h soak)
Cook Time25 mins
Total Time45 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Chole: Pressure cook soaked chickpeas with tea bag and salt. Strain tea bag.

2

Gravy: Cook onion-tomato paste in oil. Add ginger, chole masala, chili, and salt. Sauté until oil oozes.

3

Stir in chickpeas and a cup of cooking liquid. Simmer for 15 minutes until curry is thick and dark.

4

Bhature: Knead flour, curd, baking powder, and water. Rest for 2 hours. Roll into large ovals.

5

Deep fry in smoking hot oil, pressing down gently until puffed and crispy golden. Serve hot with chole.

Shahi Vegetable Biryani

Prep Time20 mins
Cook Time35 mins
Total Time55 mins
Servings6 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Sauté vegetables with biryani masala, curd, mint, and salt in ghee until 80% cooked.

2

In a heavy-bottomed pot (handi), layer half the cooked vegetables, top with half the boiled basmati rice.

3

Repeat layering. Drizzle saffron milk, ghee, mint, fried onions, and roasted cashews on top.

4

Seal the pot edges with wheat dough and place lid tightly. Cook on very low heat (dum) for 15-20 minutes.

5

Fluff gently and serve aromatic biryani hot with yogurt raita.

Lasooni Palak Paneer

Prep Time15 mins
Cook Time15 mins
Total Time30 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Blanch spinach in boiling water for 2 minutes, plunge in ice water to lock green color, and blend to a smooth puree with green chili.

2

Heat butter and oil. Crackle cumin, then sauté chopped onion and half of the minced garlic until golden.

3

Pour in spinach puree, add salt, and cook for 5 minutes. Fold in paneer cubes gently.

4

Prepare tempering: Sauté remaining minced garlic and a dried red chili in ghee. Pour hot over the curry.

5

Garnish with a swirl of fresh cream. Serve with hot tandoori roti.

Baingan Bharta & Bajra

Prep Time15 mins
Cook Time25 mins
Total Time40 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Slit eggplant, insert garlic cloves, brush with oil, and roast over an open flame until skin is charred and flesh is tender.

2

Cool, peel off charred skin, and mash roasted pulp thoroughly.

3

Sauté chopped onions, ginger-chili paste, tomatoes, and peas in oil. Add spices and cook until soft.

4

Stir in mashed eggplant, cook for 5 minutes, finish with coriander.

5

Bajra Roti: Knead warm water into millet flour, pat flat on a board, cook on a hot tawa. Smear with ghee and serve warm with bharta.

Spicy Kadai Mushroom

Prep Time15 mins
Cook Time20 mins
Total Time35 mins
Servings4 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Dry roast coriander, fennel, and red chilies, then grind to make fresh Kadai Masala.

2

Sauté cubed bell pepper and onions in hot oil for 2 minutes until crunchy, and set aside.

3

In the same pan, add mushrooms, cook until dry, then add onion-tomato gravy paste and kadai masala.

4

Fold in sautéed bell peppers and onions. Simmer for 5 minutes. Sprinkle cream and crushed kasuri methi.

5

Serve hot alongside flaky, multi-layered wheat Lachha Parathas.

Moong Dal Khichdi & Kadhi

Prep Time10 mins
Cook Time15 mins
Total Time25 mins
Servings3 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1

Wash rice and dal together. Heat ghee in pressure cooker, add cumin, hing, turmeric, dal, rice, and salt. Sauté for a minute.

2

Add 4.5 cups water and pressure cook for 3 whistles until fully soft and mashed.

3

Kadhi: Whisk sour curd, gram flour (besan), water, turmeric, and ginger-chili paste. Cook on low heat, stirring constantly until it boils.

4

Kadhi Tempering: Heat ghee, crackle mustard, cloves, cinnamon, and curry leaves. Pour immediately into the simmering Kadhi.

5

Serve hot steaming khichdi topped with a big dollop of ghee, along with kadhi, papadum, and pickles.
